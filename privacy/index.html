Privacy policy (English)

In short. Samedicino Barati is an Android app that stores, on your phone, the health information you type in yourself. It has no internet access, no account, no server and no analytics. Nothing leaves the phone unless you share it.

What is stored

Profiles (name, birth date, blood type, sex, height, pregnancy and delivery counts, relation); card records (medications, allergies, conditions, procedures, vaccines, child development milestones, an emergency contact); documents you add (photos and PDFs); readings (blood pressure, pulse, glucose, temperature, weight) with their dates; a log of when you granted or withdrew consent; and settings. Everything is entered by you. The app does not read the contents of files, recognise text, or interpret any value.

Where it is stored and how it is protected

Only on your device, in an encrypted database and encrypted files. The key lives in the Android Keystore and is bound to the phone's screen lock; the app does not run without one. Android backup and device-to-device transfer, screenshots and screen recording are switched off.

What does not happen

The app has no internet permission and never transmits data. There is no account, server, sync or cloud. There are no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reports. It asks for no camera, files, location or contacts permission: the phone's own camera app takes the photo and hands over one file.

What leaves the phone, and when

Only by your action: a password-protected encrypted backup file (.mbk), whose password is never stored and cannot be recovered; the card as a PDF or picture, without a password, for a doctor or for printing; or a single document from the share menu. Each opens the Android share sheet and you choose the destination. A shared file is then in the receiving app's control.

Reminders

With your permission the app schedules local reminders (update the card, a vaccine date). Their text carries no name, vaccine or other health data. They can be switched off in settings.

Other people's data

You may create a profile for a family member, including a child. You are responsible for entering it and for its accuracy. It is stored under the same rules as your own.

Deletion

Any record, document or profile can be deleted in the app. "Delete everything" removes all data, the encryption key and the settings. Withdrawing consent erases the card, documents and readings. Uninstalling removes everything. A backup file stays wherever you saved it.
